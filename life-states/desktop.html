01 / The Model
Six things.
One life.
Think about what actually determines how someone's life is going. Boil it all the way down and you get six things: Health, Energy, Money, Time, Network, and Agency.

They flow into each other. Good health gives you energy. Energy earns you money or buys back your time. Money and time are interchangeable — up to a point. Both open doors to people, and people give you real choices.

The arrows looping back from Money and Time up to Health are the ones most people forget. How you use your resources either maintains you — or quietly erodes you.

02 / The Glyph
Every state has
a shape.
Each life state is drawn as a small version of that same diagram — but with the lines thickened or removed depending on how strongly each flow is operating.

No line means that connection is completely dead. A hair-thin line means it barely exists. A thick line means it dominates everything else.

The two horizontal lines between Money and Time are special — they show how freely you can swap one for the other. A lawyer billing 2,800 hours a year has those rails maxed out. That's the trap. A retiree with passive income has them gone entirely. That's the freedom.

03 / The Story
26 states.
One human arc.
The 26 states are arranged left to right, from nothing to everything. But life doesn't move in a straight line — there are traps you can get stuck in, detours that look like progress, and regressions that can happen to anyone.

The most important state in the whole map isn't Peak State at the far right. It's The Pivot — the moment someone consciously decides to dismantle what isn't working and rebuild. Almost every trap has a path to it.

Every state comes with a real example: a person, a situation, a moment you'll recognise. The abstractions are meant to be grounded.

04 / The Diagram
See where
you can go.
The map at the bottom isn't just decoration — it shows you the actual transitions between states. When you select one, everything lights up: states you could realistically move to glow in colour, states out of reach fade back.

Set a goal state in Compare mode and a gold path appears, tracing the route between where you are and where you want to be. The diagram tells you which states you need to pass through — and which ones are dead ends without a Pivot first.

Arrow keys move you through the diagram spatially — it finds the nearest state in whichever direction you press.
COMPARE
Hit Compare, pick a state you're aiming for, and set it as your goal. You'll get a side-by-side diff showing exactly which flows need to grow and which need to shrink.
FILMSTRIP
Scroll the strip at the very bottom to scan all 26 glyphs at once. Patterns that are hard to describe in words become obvious visually.
CLICK NODE
Click any node directly in the diagram to jump there — everything updates instantly: the glyph, the example, the edge highlights.
