Who am I and how I spend my time

The kind of engineer who shims superconducting magnets to 2 parts per billion, then writes a natural language compiler that cuts LLM-inference cost by 100,000×.

If life is a latent space, movement is the only reward.

Current obsessions: nanoscale energy harvesting, far-field wireless power transfer, and monolithic integration—think Big Hero 6 microbots, smaller. Also shipping a natural language compiler that cuts LLM-inference cost by ~100,000×.

Engineering by nature requires constraints; and I love working with systems with the hardest ones. I've shimmed superconducting magnets for MRI at GE, designed flight controllers for eVTOL aircraft, built wireless sensor networks that detect poachers in real-time, and written compilers that turn plain English into verified code. I theorize, model, experiment and make observations to bring things to life.

Research & Ideas

SOFTWARE THEORY

NLC — Natural Language Compiler

A framework for making LLM-generated code deterministic and verifiable. Each algorithmic step is specified as a domain–range contract: the LLM generates code, the system asserts correctness, and regenerates on failure.

↓ Read Paper
SOFTWARE THEORY

The Periodic Table of Software (PSI)

All computational behavior reduces to ~650 structural primitives—analogous to the periodic table's 118 elements. Working prototype: 35 indexed fragments with contracts, deterministic SHA-256 caching, and 5 orders of magnitude energy savings.

↓ Case Study
ECONOMICS & LABOR

The Future of Software Labor

A book-length research essay analyzing 260 years of labor transitions through the lens of AI-driven automation. Central finding: when a capability becomes cheap, labor migrates one abstraction layer up.

↓ Read Essay
CONSERVATION TECH

Wireless Sensor Networks for Wildlife

Sensor nodes using 4-element microphone arrays with 2° angular discrimination, spectrogram-based noise removal, and novel PSD estimation via abstract algebra. Detects poaching activity in real-time across protected habitats.

POWER SYSTEMS

Real-Time Thermal Rating of Transmission Lines

R&D system calculating real-time ampacity based on air/conductor temperature, wind speed, and solar heating. Includes predictive models for ampacity forecasting. Published at IEEE PowerCon 2024.
